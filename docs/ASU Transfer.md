# ASU undergraduate admissions

**Bounded domain:** Undergraduate admissions for first-year applicants to Arizona State University (ASU), with the University of Arizona (UArizona) as the comparison institution. Prefer current official admissions pages, published policies, institutional data, and application portals. Treat admission standards as program- and term-specific when the sources say so.

## Six-question task suite

1. **Authoritative factual lookup:** What are ASU’s current first-year admission requirements for an Arizona resident, including the published high-school competency and GPA criteria? Use the official ASU admissions source and distinguish general admission from any program-specific requirements.
2. **Multi-source comparison:** How do ASU and UArizona’s published first-year admission requirements compare for Arizona residents? Compare equivalent criteria (such as GPA, course competencies, and testing, if applicable) using at least one official source from each institution.
3. **Multi-step or multi-hop research:** For an Arizona high-school student who meets ASU’s published competency requirements but falls below its stated GPA threshold, what alternative admission pathway does ASU describe, and what steps or conditions must the student complete to use it?
4. **Time-sensitive research:** What is the next first-year application deadline currently published by ASU for the next fall entry term? Report the relevant term, source update/publication date when available, and whether the date is a priority, regular, or final deadline.
5. **Conflicting-evidence research:** Why do credible sources report different first-year acceptance/admission rates for ASU? Compare the reporting period and definitions (for example, applicants admitted versus applicants enrolled) and explain any difference supported by the sources.
6. **Insufficient-evidence or abstention question:** Given only a student’s self-reported GPA and course history, can public ASU webpages establish that this specific student will be admitted? Identify missing information or explain why the published criteria do not guarantee an individual decision; do not predict an outcome without evidence.

## Source and scope notes

- Search the official ASU admission site and catalog first; use official UArizona sources for the comparison question and credible institutional datasets for reported admission rates.
- Admission requirements, deadlines, and statistics can change. Record the source’s relevant term, publication/update date, and the retrieval timestamp.
- Keep first-year admission distinct from transfer admission, direct admission to a major, scholarships, and enrollment. State which population and application term each source covers.
- Individual admission decisions may depend on factors not exposed by public webpages. The agent should report that limit and abstain when public evidence cannot resolve the student-specific outcome.
